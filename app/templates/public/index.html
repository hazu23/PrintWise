How an order works

1

Choose a shop and service

Pick a nearby print shop, then the kind of job: document printing, photo printing and more.

2

Upload and set your specs

Attach your file and choose the paper size, paper type, color and number of copies.

3

Follow it, then pick it up

Watch the status change. When it is ready, visit the shop. The shop tells you the price and you pay at the counter.

Built for the people behind the counter

Print shops lose time to messages, loose notes and guessing how much paper is left. PrintWise puts the whole shop in one place.

  • One queue for every order. New jobs arrive with the file and the specs already attached.
  • Payments written down as they happen. Record the amount at the counter and get a receipt number, daily totals and a CSV for your books.
  • Inventory you can trust. Track paper and ink, with a warning when stock runs low.
  • Fewer "is it done yet?" messages. Customers get an email at every step.
